Voice & privacy

Voice starts only when you click Start. Your browser’s speech service may process microphone audio remotely; transcripts are sent through this site’s server to DeepSeek. Replies use your device/browser’s voice, not a clone. This page stores no conversation after refresh.

Voice recognition works only in supported browsers (try Chrome or Edge). Speaker mode pauses recognition during playback to reduce echo; click Interrupt to talk. Headphone mode keeps listening, but can mistake noise or echo for an interruption. Text always remains available. Sessions end after 10 minutes and pause when this tab is hidden.
