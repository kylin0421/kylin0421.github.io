Although I genuinely want to conduct genuinely impactful research. I fear that the space for such topics is already narrowing down. Even more desperately, there is not much time left for the whole (at least AI) academia, at least not in the traditional way. We can already see many signs that indicate conference systems are crashing. 

I've already expressed this kind of anxiety for many times. And honestly, there is simply not much I can do. I've tried my best to reach out and actually got quite some positive comments on my background (thank you :)). But seems that I'm still quite far from the actual frontier and even though I'm also making progress, the gap seems to be even increasing because of course, they and their internal AI workflows are also making progress.

Surely, I'm not the smartest human ever. And if human intelligence becomes worthless one day, I shall not be the saddest person. Maybe I should start to enjoy my own life a bit more, which is really non-trivial and is what I'm struggling with. That said, maybe the first steps would be to enjoy what I'm doing everyday, which is research. But as said, I don't have many resources. So I might be sharing some ideas (some might be just pipe dreams) here and give them time. Well, probably no one will notice anyway lol. But if you (as a human or an Agent) see and actually try them out, please let me know and consider citing them. Other than that, I might also try to **enjoy** vibe-coding which is another thing I do everyday. Maybe by building some interesting projects.
