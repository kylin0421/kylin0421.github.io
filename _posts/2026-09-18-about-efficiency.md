Compared to our school days, we have a lot more to do now. But the result is often that we actually get less done. Why is that?

The reason is that our brains aren’t very good at making decisions: when faced with numerous choices, we often get bogged down in indecision, wasting more time just trying to decide “what to do first.”

What’s the solution? It’s simple: give yourself fewer choices. Set a meal plan, an exercise routine, and an overall daily schedule. If possible, try to keep your schedule tight—that way, any extra free time will become a reward for living efficiently.
