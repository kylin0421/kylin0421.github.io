RSI requires complex capabilities from different aspects. Yet it might not necessarily be that hard as we might think e.g. abstract math reasoning, extensive world knowledge, very reliable engineering capability. Rather, it might be more achievable if we formulate the problem in a highly structured yet not over-simplified way.

Though several ingredients are still missing. For example highly reliable verification of trial. Maybe we'll need efficient yet dynamic benchmark for this which may in itself be an ill-posed or even impossible task. To measure intelligence from some aspects is feasible, yet it's a lot more tricky to try to measure intelligence itself. It's more of a philosophical problem.
