RSI requires complex capabilities from different aspects. Yet it might not necessarily be that hard as we might think e.g. abstract math reasoning, extensive world knowledge, very reliable engineering capability. Rather, it might be more achievable if we formulate the problem in a highly structured yet not over-simplified way.
